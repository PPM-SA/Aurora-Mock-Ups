Choosing a sitting desk to standing desk solution for your commercial workplace

Height-adjustable sit-stand desks have moved well beyond a wellness trend — they're now standard specification in most commercial office fitouts across Canberra, Sydney and Melbourne. But not all sit-stand desks are the same, and choosing the wrong system creates problems that are expensive to fix after installation.

The most common mistake Aurora sees is buyers selecting consumer-grade desks without understanding the difference in duty rating, warranty coverage or long-term reliability under daily commercial use.

What makes a sit-stand desk commercially suitable? Commercial-grade height-adjustable desks are duty-rated for 8 hours of daily use, carry meaningful manufacturer warranties (typically 5–10 years on the frame and motor), and meet Australian commercial compliance and AFRDI testing requirements. Consumer desks are typically rated for 1–2 hours of use per day and carry warranties of 12 months or less.

Single motor vs dual motor — what's the practical difference? For most standard workstations, a single-motor system provides sufficient lift capacity and is entirely appropriate. Dual-motor systems are specified where the desk carries a heavier load or where the desk is used in high-frequency environments where cycle speed and reliability matter most.

Why cable management should be part of the specification, not an afterthought. A moving desk surface changes the requirements entirely — cables need to flex, power access needs to travel with the worksurface, and the result needs to look professional. Aurora integrates cable management and power solutions at the point of product selection.
